For residents

Delete your RepLiT account

Your account is frozen for 30 days first. In those 30 days you can still restore it; after them it is deleted for good.

What happens

  • Right awayYou are signed out on every phone, and RepLiT stops sending you alerts.
  • For 30 daysYour account is kept, frozen. To keep it, sign in to the app and choose Restore my account, or use Restore your account below.
  • After 30 daysYour sign-in and profile (name, email, mobile number, birthday, gender, language and last location), your alerts and any National ID photos are deleted for good.
  • What staysThe reports you sent stay in the barangay's incident records, without your name.

Staff accounts (coordinators and responders) are removed by their organization's administrator.
