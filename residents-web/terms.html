RepLiT Terms

Effective 26 November 2026

RepLiT is an emergency reporting app for Barangay 76 Zone 10, Pasay City, developed by M2STZ Crew. These terms apply to everyone who uses it: residents, the organizations affiliated with RepLiT, and their staff. By creating an account or applying as an organization, you agree to them. How RepLiT handles your information is in the privacy policy.

In an emergency

  • RepLiT helps bring your report to the responders who can help. It does not replace emergency hotlines: when a life is in danger, call them too.
  • RepLiT depends on your phone, your signal and the internet. We work to keep it running but cannot promise that it is always available or that every report reaches someone in time.

Your account

  • Use your real name, as on your ID, and your own email address. One account per person.
  • Keep your password to yourself. Your account works on one phone at a time.
  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.

Reports

  • Report only real emergencies, with a true photo and your real location.
  • False and prank reports send responders away from real emergencies. They pause your reporting, can remove your account (see the privacy policy), and may be reported to the authorities.

Organizations and their staff

  • An organization's application must be true and complete, including its SEC certificate. The central hub reviews every application and may decline it.
  • Staff accounts are personal: use only your own, and only for your organization's work on RepLiT.
  • Act only within your role: going to a fire, saying what is burning, asking for or setting the alarm level, and marking fire out are recorded with your name.
  • An organization that misuses RepLiT can be suspended or removed from the network by the central hub.

Respect

  • Do not harass anyone, share other people's information, or use what you see in RepLiT for anything but the emergency at hand.
  • Do not try to break, overload or get around RepLiT's security.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time from the app or on this site. We may pause or remove an account that breaks these terms.

Changes

Changes are posted on this page with a new date. Important ones are also announced in the app.

Contact

For questions about these terms, contact barangay76.pasay@gmail.com, or the Barangay 76 Zone 10 hall, Pasay City.
