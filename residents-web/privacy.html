RepLiT Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

RepLiT is an emergency reporting app for Barangay 76 Zone 10, Pasay City. Residents report fires and other emergencies with a photo and their location, and the barangay and the responder organizations affiliated with RepLiT (fire volunteers, the Bureau of Fire Protection, the Philippine National Police and medical teams) receive them and respond. This policy says what RepLiT collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept, and how to delete your account. It follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). RepLiT is developed by M2STZ Crew.

What RepLiT collects

  • Your account: your name as on your ID, email address, password (kept only in scrambled form by our sign-in provider), and, if you give them, your mobile number, date of birth and gender, and the language you choose.
  • Verification: confirmation of your email. If you add your National ID: photos of your PhilID or ePhilID, its QR code and a selfie, taken with the camera in the app. An administrator checks the QR code on the Philippine Statistics Authority's PhilSys Check. The photos are deleted as soon as the review is decided.
  • Your reports: the photo you take, the time, your location and how accurate it is, the agencies you ask for, what you write, and your trust level at that moment.
  • Location: while you use the app, to place your report and to show incidents near you. If you allow location "All the time" (optional), the app sends your location about every 15 minutes, only to warn you of a fire within 100 meters. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Responders' location: a responder's position is shared every 5 seconds while they go to an incident, until it is marked fire out.
  • Your phone: a notification token, the app version and the phone model, to send you alerts.
  • Your answers to alerts, such as whether you can see a fire reported near you.
  • Records of staff and administrator actions (who did what and when, with the internet address used), so that every decision can be accounted for.

How it is used

  • To bring your report to the responders who can help, and to coordinate them.
  • To tell you how your report is going, and to warn you of fires near you.
  • To show responders how far an account is verified, so they can judge a report.
  • To keep the barangay's record of incidents and the teams' post-incident reports.
  • To keep RepLiT safe from misuse, for example by refusing temporary email addresses, and by pausing reports after prank reports (see False and prank reports).

RepLiT does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Responders and coordinators of the agencies your report asks for, and the barangay: what they need to respond, including your report, its photo and location, your name and your trust level.
  • Other residents never see your name, contact details or photos. They may see that an incident is active near them, where, and how it is going.
  • Administrators of the barangay and the affiliated organizations, to manage accounts and to review National IDs.
  • Service providers that run RepLiT, only for that purpose: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Render (server), Vercel (websites), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), Brevo (email), DeepSeek (drafts incident summaries from the teams' reports; residents' names and contact details are not sent), and Mapbox and OpenStreetMap (map images, which see your device's internet address and the area shown).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

These providers' servers may be outside the Philippines.

How long it is kept

  • Your account: until you delete it. When you ask to delete it, it is frozen for 30 days, then deleted.
  • National ID photos: until the review is decided, then deleted.
  • Reports and incident records: kept as the barangay's record of emergencies, also after your account is deleted, without your name.
  • Your alerts and your phones' registrations: deleted with your account.
  • Records of staff and administrator actions: kept for accountability.
  • Prank-report strikes: 12 months after each takes effect (see False and prank reports).

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete my account. On the web: reportwhatslit-app.vercel.app/delete-account.

  • Right away your account is frozen: you are signed out on every phone and get no alerts.
  • For 30 days you can restore it: sign in to the app and choose Restore my account, or use the same page.
  • After 30 days your sign-in, profile, alerts, phones' registrations, verification records and any National ID photos are deleted for good. The reports you sent stay, without your name.

A staff account is removed by its organization's administrator.

False and prank reports

Giving a false or malicious fire alarm is against the Fire Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 9514, Section 8(i)). When staff find that a report was a prank, and never for an honest mistake, its reporter gets a strike and their reporting pauses: 24 hours the first time, then 7 days, then 30 days, then no reporting until an appeal is granted. Strikes count over 12 months. Calling 911, getting fire alerts and signing in are never blocked.

  • What is kept: the report it concerns, what staff found, the dates, and a scrambled form of your verified mobile number, so a strike stays with the number even on a new account.
  • Appeals: you can appeal a strike in the app within 7 days. Someone other than the person who gave it decides.
  • How long: a strike is deleted 12 months after it takes effect, and one removed on appeal 30 days after. A 4th strike stays until an appeal is granted.
  • Authorities: repeated or serious prank reports may be referred to the Bureau of Fire Protection or the barangay.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may be informed, see your data, correct it (Profile, then Edit profile), object, have it deleted (delete your account), and get a copy of it. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Age

RepLiT accounts are for people 18 and over.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Staff see only what their role needs, and every staff and administrator action is recorded.

Changes

Changes are posted on this page with a new date. Important ones are also announced in the app.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, contact barangay76.pasay@gmail.com, or the Barangay 76 Zone 10 hall, Pasay City.
